CASE STUDY 02 · QUALITATIVE CUSTOMER DISCOVERY & JOURNEY MAPPING

Slow Living Brand: Mapping how consumers move from digital fatigue to mindful rituals.

Through structured online discussions and qualitative interviews, we investigated why many "wellness" brands feel performative—and mapped the six stages required to earn genuine routine adoption.

PROJECT CLASSIFICATIONMBA Marketing Research Project (2026)
METHODSQualitative Interviews · Persona Synthesis · Journey Mapping
MY CONTRIBUTIONInterview Discovery, Insight Synthesis & 6-Stage Journey Map
STATUSCompleted Academic Study
ARTIFACT 02 — SIX-STAGE CUSTOMER JOURNEY & EMOTIONAL FRICTION MAP QUALITATIVE SYNTHESIS ARTIFACT
01 · AWARENESS

Burnout Trigger

Notices screen fatigue; drawn to calm, unhurried visual storytelling.

02 · INTEREST

Provenance Check

Looks for tactile honesty and material transparency over hype.

03 · CONSIDERATION

Utility Friction

Questions: "Will I actually use this on a busy weekday night?"

04 · PURCHASE

Calm Conversion

Reassured by simple starter ritual guides & honest pricing.

05 · EXPERIENCE

Tactile Ritual

Sensory unboxing anchors a 10-minute offline wind-down habit.

06 · LOYALTY

Quiet Advocacy

Sustained via low-waste refills and peer word-of-mouth.

01 & 02 / CHALLENGE & INSIGHT

The gap between aspiration and Tuesday night

Consumers love the idea of slow living on Sunday morning, but abandon complex routines by Tuesday evening. Our interviews revealed that simplicity of habit integration is the real purchase barrier.

03 & 04 / CONTRIBUTION & FRAMEWORK

From raw transcripts to structured personas

Participated in qualitative online discussions and interviews, coding recurring lifestyle pain points into actionable customer personas and target segments.

05–07 / OUTCOME & LEARNING

Designing for the Consideration drop-off

By pinpointing Stage 03 (Consideration) as the highest skepticism point, we recommended shifting brand messaging from abstract mindfulness to concrete, 5-minute daily rituals.
