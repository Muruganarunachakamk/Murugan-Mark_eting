CASE STUDY 01 · SEGMENTATION, TARGETING & POSITIONING

Gravity Shift: Market-entry strategy for a load-reducing smart backpack.

Hardware innovations fail when marketed to "everyone who carries a bag." Through exploratory pain-point discovery, I isolated which everyday carry segments experience enough spinal strain to justify a premium smart-suspension price tier.

PROJECT CLASSIFICATIONAcademic Marketing Strategy Concept (MBA, 2026)
CORE FRAMEWORKSExploratory Research · Needs-Based STP · 4Ps Mix
MY ROLEConsumer Pain-Point Analysis, Segmentation & Positioning
STATUSCompleted Academic Deliverable
ARTIFACT 01 — NEEDS-BASED SEGMENTATION & WILLINGNESS-TO-PAY (WTP) ARCHITECTURE ILLUSTRATIVE STRATEGY ARTIFACT
01 / THE CHALLENGE

Avoiding the "for everyone" trap

A smart backpack with load-reducing suspension carries higher manufacturing costs than a standard bag. Launching without sharp segmentation risks pricing out students while failing to convince professionals.

02 / THE INSIGHT QUESTION

Who pays to eliminate weight?

Which consumer groups experience daily carry strain severe enough that ergonomic load reduction shifts from a "nice gadget" to an essential health and workflow investment?

03 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Exploratory research & STP

Led the exploratory inquiry into user carry routines, pain points, and willingness to pay, then structured the needs-based segmentation, target prioritization, and 4Ps market-entry mix.

04 & 05 / FRAMEWORK & STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATION

Positioning around "Unfelt Weight" for urban commuters first

By cross-referencing physical strain duration against purchasing power, we prioritized Urban Commuter Professionals as the beachhead segment, positioning Gravity Shift around spinal longevity and sleek executive utility, supported by direct-to-consumer digital storytelling and ergonomic retail trial zones.

06 & 07 / DELIVERABLE, STATUS & LEARNING

What this academic strategy project taught me

Demographics ("ages 20–35") mask the real purchase trigger. Segmenting by load context and physical consequence immediately clarified how product tiers, price points, and channel choices must align.
