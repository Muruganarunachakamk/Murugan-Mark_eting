CASE STUDY 03 · EMPIRICAL SURVEY & INFERENTIAL STATISTICS

The Role of Advertisement Media on Consumer Buying Behaviour.

My first primary consumer study: surveying 100+ respondents and applying Chi-Square ($\chi^2$) and One-Way ANOVA in Microsoft Excel to test how advertising channels shape purchase decisions across demographics.

PROJECT CLASSIFICATIONUndergraduate Dissertation · Sri Krishna Arts & Science College
SAMPLE & TOOLSN = 100+ Primary Survey · Microsoft Excel
STATISTICAL METHODSChi-Square ($\chi^2$) Test of Independence · One-Way ANOVA
STATUSCompleted Primary Research
ARTIFACT 03 — EMPIRICAL HYPOTHESIS-TESTING ARCHITECTURE STATISTICAL WORKFLOW
01 & 02 / QUESTION & CONTEXT

Separating channel hype from buying influence

Do digital and traditional advertising channels influence recall, trust, and final purchase decisions differently across demographic cohorts?

03 & 04 / METHOD & EXECUTION

Inferential discipline over pie charts

Designed the primary questionnaire, collected 100+ responses, cleaned the dataset in Excel, and ran Chi-Square and One-Way ANOVA tests to evaluate statistical significance.

05–07 / INSIGHT & TRAJECTORY

The spark for my MBA research path

This dissertation sparked my fascination with consumer psychology and directly set the foundation for the structural equation modeling I now pursue in my MBA BRL research.
