Pragati PR: Testing value propositions live through outbound institutional outreach.

As a Public Relations Committee Member for Pragati at Amrita School of Business, I execute direct outbound calling, institutional pitching, and multi-college participant coordination.

ROLE & INSTITUTIONPR Committee Member · Pragati, Amrita School of Business
CORE ACTIVITIESCold Calling · Institutional Pitching · Event Coordination
TIMELINE2026 – Present
STATUSActive Leadership & Outreach Role
01 / THE CHALLENGE

Breaking through institutional inbox fatigue

Colleges receive dozens of generic event posters every week. Securing real delegation participation requires direct human outreach to faculty gatekeepers and student coordinators.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Cold-calling & stakeholder pipeline

Conducted direct cold calls to target colleges, tailored the pitch to each department’s academic calendar, answered eligibility queries, and coordinated follow-up registration logistics.

03 / MARKETING TAKEAWAY

Ten-second message validation

Outbound calling is the fastest copywriting classroom in marketing: you learn immediately which benefit hooks attention and which phrasing causes a prospect to hesitate.
